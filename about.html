About us

Docu-Magic is a client-side PDF and image toolkit. Work stays on your device.

We built Docu-Magic so you can merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign, and protect files without uploading them to a server. Open a tool, drop a file, and download the result in your browser.

What we believe

Your documents are yours. Processing in the browser means files never leave your device for our tools to work. No account is required to use the editor.

What you can do

  • Organize PDFs: merge, split, extract, remove, and reorder files.
  • Optimize: compress or repair a PDF.
  • Convert: PDF to JPG, JPG to PDF, HTML to PDF, and PDF to Word-friendly text.
  • Edit: text, images, rotate, crop, watermark, and page numbers.
  • Secure: sign, protect, unlock, and redact.
  • Images: resize, compress to a target size in KB, and change dimensions in px, inch, cm, or mm.
