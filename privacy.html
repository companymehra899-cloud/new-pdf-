Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Docu-Magic is a client-side PDF and image editor. Files you open in the tools are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers.

Files you process

PDFs and images stay on your device. We do not receive, store, or look at the contents of files you merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign, or download.

Local storage

The workspace may keep a short-lived copy of a selected file in your browser (for example IndexedDB or session storage) so the tool can open. That data stays on your device and can be cleared with your browser settings.

Usage data

This site does not require an account. We do not sell personal data. Standard hosting or preview logs may record technical details such as IP address and pages requested.

Cookies

Docu-Magic does not use advertising cookies. The app may use session storage only to remember the tool you opened.

Questions about this policy: Contact us.
